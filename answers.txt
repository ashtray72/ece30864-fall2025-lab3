(a) Agile allows for a lot of flexibility in the software development lifecycle. It also allows for continuous customer feedback on the product.
    This flexibility can also lead to prolonged development times and varying quality of the product during the iterative process.

(b) The scrum master manages all scrum related tasks: JIRA tooling, sprints, and meetings. While the product owner sets the team's direction 
    and handles releases and stockholders.

(c) Sprint planning meeting: Determine what tasks are being included in the sprint, determine story points and and assign stories to teams.
    Daily Scrum/Standup meeting: Discuss work completed and the work plan for the day.
    Sprint Retrospective meeting: Discuss tasks completed and move incompleted work to backlog.

(d) An epic consists of a large body of work that can be broken down into smaller stories.
    Stories express a software system requirement in a few short sentences.
    Bugs represent a problem that needs to be fixed.
    Tasks are work tasks that need to be done, used as a default when other issue types don't fit.
